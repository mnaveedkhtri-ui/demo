The house

Grilled over charcoal. Grown in a garden.

Kabab Ghar Garden sits behind Sunny CNG on Main Wadhu Wah Road — a family-run dining room and open-air garden built around a charcoal grill. The kitchen runs wide: smoky Sindhi karahi and BBQ tikka on one end, Chinese wok dishes and continental steaks on the other, with a full drinks bar of mojitos, margaritas and iced coffee in between.

Every table is a family table. Come hungry, stay for the ambiance.
